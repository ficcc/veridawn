Veridawn — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 November 2026. Contact: veridawn.app@gmail.com.

Veridawn plans photos around the sun and the moon. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your photos, scenes and plans stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location. The app uses it on the phone to work out where the sun and the moon will be. Each scene you save stores where it was taken. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • Photos, scenes, plans and settings. They are kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Map, elevation and geocoder requests reveal roughly which area you are looking at, as any map or web request would. The developer receives none of them.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take scene photos.
  • Location (precise): to place the sun correctly where you stand.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: for plan reminders. You can say no; plans still work without reminders.
  • Internet: for map tiles, elevation data and place names.

Backups

If Android backup is on, your settings, scenes and plans are part of your own Google account backup, under Google's terms. Downloaded terrain is excluded; it can be downloaded again.

No tracking

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting to any server.

Web version

The web version of Veridawn works the same way, with these differences:

  • Place names. When Settings → Name places is on, a place's coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look up its name.
  • Terrain and maps. Terrain comes from AWS Terrain Tiles (Amazon S3, the elevation-tiles-prod bucket), and map tiles from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. The requests show which area is being downloaded.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google), which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses.
  • Fonts. Some symbol characters are drawn with fonts fetched from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com); those requests show your IP address to Google. Once fetched, the fonts are kept in the browser.
  • Your data. Everything you save stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is backed up unless you make a file with Settings → Back up.
  • No tracking. There is no analytics or tracking. The diagnostics screen stays on the device; it is copied only if you tap Copy.

This website

  • Video. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play on the home page. The video then plays from YouTube (a Google service), which sees your IP address and applies Google's terms.
  • Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
