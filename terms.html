Predictions are a guide

Sun, moon and Milky Way positions, times and alignments are calculated from astronomical models, your phone's sensors, map data and elevation data. They are usually close, but compass readings can be off, terrain data can be coarse, and weather is not part of the prediction. Treat every result as a guide and check the scene yourself.
