Veridawn

Plan photos around the sun, the moon and the Milky Way.

Stand where you'll shoot and take a photo: Veridawn remembers where the camera pointed. Drag through the day, or through the year, and watch the sun and the moon cross your photo, behind the real horizon.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • A map with the sun and moon directions for any day, a compass ring and a 24-hour dial.
  • Golden hour, blue hour and twilight times, with sunrise and sunset behind the real terrain.
  • The dates the sun or the moon lines up with a peak, a tower or a lighthouse.
  • The best nights for the Milky Way, reminders before a plan, and a home-screen widget.
  • Photographer's tools: depth of field, hyperfocal distance, exposure, star trails, time-lapse and more.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your photos, scenes and plans stay on your phone. Privacy policy.

Support Veridawn
